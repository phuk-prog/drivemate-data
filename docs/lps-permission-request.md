# Draft letter: permission to include Northern Ireland postcode locations

**To:** Land & Property Services (LPS), Intellectual Property / Mapping Helpdesk
**Email:** mapping.helpdesk@finance-ni.gov.uk
**Subject:** Permission request: Northern Ireland postcode centroids in a free offline navigation app

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am developing DriveMate, a free satellite-navigation app for Android, currently for my personal use and possibly to be offered free of charge on the Google Play Store later. The app can search for places with no mobile signal using a small offline index.

For Great Britain the index uses Ordnance Survey's OS Open Names under the Open Government Licence. For Northern Ireland I already use the OSNI Open Data Gazetteer (street and place names) under the Open Government Licence, with the required acknowledgement.

I would like to also include the location (a single latitude and longitude per postcode) of Northern Ireland postcodes, as found in the BT records of the ONS Postcode Directory. The ONS licence states that use of the Northern Ireland data beyond internal business use requires a licence from LPS. I am therefore writing to ask:

1. May I include Northern Ireland postcode centroids (postcode plus one coordinate, with no address or property details) in the app's offline search file, which is distributed to users of the app free of charge?
2. If so, what licence terms, acknowledgement wording and any fee would apply?
3. Could you also confirm that the OSNI Open Data Gazetteer datasets (Streetnames and Place Names), which are marked as available under the Open Government Licence, may be included in the app on that basis?

I will not include any Northern Ireland postcode data until I have your written permission. Thank you for your help.

Yours faithfully,

[Your name]
[Contact email]
